📖 How Watch & Earn Works

A simple guide explaining how to complete YouTube video tasks and understand your available rewards.

Welcome to Watch & Earn!
The working process is simple. Registered users can complete assigned YouTube video tasks and receive rewards according to the number of eligible videos they complete.
1

🎬 Watch Daily Videos

You can complete a maximum of 10 eligible videos per day.

  • Reward per video: Rs. 100
  • Maximum videos per day: 10
  • Maximum daily reward: Rs. 1,000
Rs. 100 1 Video
Rs. 500 5 Videos
Rs. 1,000 10 Videos
2

⏱️ Watch Each Video for 15 Minutes

Each assigned video must be watched for the required 15-minute duration before the task can be considered complete.

3

🎮 PUBG Gaming Content

The assigned videos are focused on PUBG gaming content.

New video tasks may be provided regularly.

4

💰 Daily Limit & Payment

Each registered Gmail account can complete up to 10 eligible videos per day.

If you complete all 10 eligible videos, the total reward is Rs. 1,000.

  • Minimum example: 1 completed video = Rs. 100
  • 5 completed videos = Rs. 500
  • 10 completed videos = Rs. 1,000

Supported payment methods may include:

  • Easypaisa
  • JazzCash
5

🌙 Watch Anytime

You can complete your available tasks at a convenient time during the day or night, provided that the tasks are still available.

6

🔗 New Links Every Day

New YouTube video links will be provided regularly. Check the Watch Tasks section of your dashboard to see the videos currently available.

7

📧 Use Your Registered Gmail Account

Your registered Gmail account is used to identify your account and track eligible activity.

⚠️ Important

Please use the same Gmail account that you registered with when completing your assigned YouTube activities. Using a different account may prevent the activity from being properly recognized.

8

💳 Registration Fee Refund Policy

✓ Refund Information

If you register with Watch & Earn but decide not to continue with the work after registration, your registration fee will be eligible for a refund according to our refund process and applicable conditions.


For refund assistance, please contact our support team.

✓

Important Guidelines

  • Complete only the videos assigned to your account.
  • Watch each required video for the full 15 minutes.
  • Use your registered Gmail account.
  • Do not submit duplicate or invalid activity.
  • Check your dashboard regularly for new tasks.
  • Follow the instructions displayed with each task.
